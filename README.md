# Coca-Cola Sales Analysis — 2021

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Coca-Cola beverage sales data across the United States in 2021 using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to evaluate sales performance, profitability, retailer contributions, regional trends, brand performance, and pricing patterns to support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools and Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning and Preparation
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel Formulas
- Conditional Formatting
- Interactive Dashboard and Slicers

## Dataset
The dataset contains 3,888 sales records from 2021, including retailer, region, state, city, beverage brand, units sold, total sales, operating profit, and operating margin.

## Key Performance Indicators
| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | $8,684,028 |
| Operating Profit | $3,173,632 |
| Operating Profit Margin | 36.55% |
| Total Units Sold | 17,148,250 |

## Analysis Performed
1. **Retailer Analysis:** Compared retailer sales, operating profit, margins, and units sold.
2. **Regional Analysis:** Analyzed sales and profitability across US regions.
3. **Brand Analysis:** Compared beverage brands by sales, profit, and sales volume.
4. **State and City Analysis:** Identified leading locations based on sales and profitability.
5. **Monthly Analysis:** Examined monthly sales trends and operating profit.
6. **Pricing Analysis:** Compared average selling prices and sales volumes across brands.

## Key Findings
- **West** was the highest-revenue region, generating $2,835,100.
- **Sodapop** was the highest-revenue retailer, generating $4,403,630.
- **Coca-Cola** was the highest-revenue beverage brand, generating $2,015,890.
- **December** recorded the highest monthly sales of $1,045,775.
- **BevCo** achieved the highest retailer operating profit margin of 41.97%.
- Among the top 10 states by revenue, **South Carolina** achieved the highest operating margin of 40.38%.

## Business Recommendations
- Maintain strong distribution in high-performing regions while investigating opportunities in lower-performing markets.
- Strengthen partnerships with leading retailers.
- Improve profitability by reviewing pricing, product mix, and operating costs.
- Plan inventory and promotional activities around peak sales periods.
- Use the dashboard to monitor regional and retailer performance.

## Dashboard
The Excel dashboard includes four KPI cards, five charts, and a Region slicer for interactive exploration of connected PivotCharts.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to transform sales data into meaningful business insights through data preparation, analysis, visualization, and dashboard development.
